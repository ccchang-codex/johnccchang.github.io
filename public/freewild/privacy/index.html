Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Freewild handles information when you use the game.

Overview

Freewild does not require an account. Puzzle progress and preferences are saved on your device. Freewild uses third-party services for advertising and optional one-time purchases, as described below.

Information stored on your device

Freewild stores your current and completed levels, rescued animals, available and used animal skills, hint and Undo use, tutorial progress, ad-break timing, and settings such as language, music, sound, and haptics locally on your device. The game does not currently send gameplay events to a Freewild-operated analytics service. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backups and platform behavior.

Advertising

The free version of Freewild uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK for occasional interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads that grant an additional hint or Undo. Freewild requests non-personalized ads. Even for non-personalized ads, Google may process information such as your IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device and advertising identifiers where available, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver, measure, secure, and improve ads.

Where required, Freewild uses Google's consent tools before requesting ads. You can decline an optional rewarded ad and continue playing. The Wild and All Wild one-time purchases remove ads. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Freewild uses Apple or Google Play billing and RevenueCat to offer and restore the one-time Wild and All Wild purchases. These providers may process transaction records, purchase status, app and device information, and identifiers needed to validate purchases, prevent fraud, and restore access. Freewild does not receive your full payment-card details. Your purchase entitlement may remain available through the store and RevenueCat after you delete the game so it can be restored.

Learn more in Apple's Privacy Policy, Google's Privacy Policy, and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Support communications

If you email Freewild Support, we receive what you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and device details. We use it to respond, troubleshoot, prevent abuse, and improve the game. Please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Sharing and retention

Freewild does not operate a service that collects your saved gameplay progress. Google, Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat may process information for the advertising and purchase purposes described above under their own policies. We may disclose support communications if required by law or to protect rights and safety. Support emails are kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and maintain appropriate records. Local gameplay data remains until you remove it or delete the app, subject to device backups.

Children's privacy

Freewild is a general-audience puzzle game and does not ask for personal information to play. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support request, contact us so we can review it and delete it where appropriate.

International processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy policies explain the safeguards and rights applicable to their processing.

Your choices

  • Choose whether to watch an optional rewarded ad.
  • Use the advertising choices presented by Google's consent message where applicable.
  • Purchase Wild or All Wild to play without ads.
  • Delete Freewild to remove locally stored gameplay data, subject to device backups.
  • Contact us about a support communication you previously sent.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Freewild, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
